Support

聯絡我們

請電郵至 climber-52-bezel@icloud.com，並簡單說明問題。除非解決問題必需，請勿寄送私人旅程內容、身分證明、付款資料或 Apple Account 密碼／驗證碼。

如要回報 App 錯誤，請附上 App 版本、iPhone 型號及 iOS 版本、出現問題嘅步驟，以及預期和實際結果。傳送電郵後，發布者會收到你選擇提供嘅寄件地址及電郵內容；請遮蓋姓名、電話及行程私人資料。

發布者會使用郵件內容回覆及處理支援要求，只會在處理所需期間保留；問題處理完畢後會刪除，除非需要持續跟進或法律要求保留更長時間。電郵服務供應商、裝置或備份中嘅副本可能按其自身規則保留。

常見問題

旅程資料儲存喺邊度？

目前版本把旅程保存在本機 App 資料中，沒有帳戶或發布者伺服器同步。若你選擇 iOS 裝置備份，系統備份可能另存副本。

我分享咗旅程，之後修改會更新嗎？

唔會。分享內容係你傳送當時嘅文字副本。更新後如要讓同行者收到新版本，你需要重新分享。

備份檔包含私人備註嗎？

會。完整 JSON 備份包含旅程及活動已保存欄位，包括備註。匯出前請選擇妥善嘅儲存位置；檔案本身沒有另外加上 App 專用密碼或加密。

清除旅程會刪除我分享出去嘅內容嗎？

唔會。清除只影響 App 內本機旅程；已傳給別人嘅內容及另存嘅備份要分別管理。

可以喺另一部手機自動看到同一行程嗎？

目前未有帳戶或多人／跨裝置同步功能。你可手動分享文字副本，或匯出備份後在另一部裝置匯入；匯入會新增一個獨立旅程。

刪除後可否向發布者申請還原資料？

發布者不會收到 App 內旅程，無法代你還原。若你先前曾匯出備份，可由自己保存嘅檔案建立新旅程。

Contact

Email climber-52-bezel@icloud.com with a short description of the issue. Do not send private trip details, identity documents, payment information, or your Apple Account password or verification code unless strictly needed to diagnose an issue.

For a bug report, include the app version, iPhone model and iOS version, steps to reproduce, and expected versus actual results. The Publisher will receive the sender address and message you choose to send. Remove names, phone numbers, and private itinerary details first.

The Publisher uses the message to respond and resolve the support request, keeps it only as long as needed, and deletes it when resolved unless the request remains open or the law requires longer retention. Email providers, devices, or backups may retain separate copies under their own rules.

Frequently asked questions

Where are my trips stored?

The current version saves trips in the app’s local data. There is no account or Publisher server sync. An iOS device backup may store a separate copy if you enable backups.

Will a shared trip update when I edit it?

No. Sharing sends a text snapshot at that time. Share again to give your travel companions an updated copy.

Does a backup contain private notes?

Yes. A full JSON backup contains the trip and saved activity fields, including notes. Choose a storage location you trust; TripOrder does not add a separate app password or encryption to the file.

Does clearing trips delete copies I shared?

No. Clear-data only affects local trips in the app. Shared copies and saved backups must be managed separately.

Can I automatically see the same trip on another phone?

There is currently no account, group, or cross-device sync. You can share a text snapshot or export a backup and import it on another device; importing creates a separate trip.

Can the Publisher restore a deleted trip?

The Publisher does not receive trips from the app and cannot restore them. If you exported a backup earlier, you can use your own saved file to create a new trip.
