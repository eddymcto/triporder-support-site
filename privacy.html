App 會處理甚麼資料

旅序讓你喺本機建立旅程。你輸入嘅旅程名稱、目的地、日期、時區、活動時間、標題、地點、航班或住宿資料及備註，會儲存在本機 App 資料中。App 亦會保存每個旅程和活動嘅識別碼、建立／修改時間，以及你選擇嘅目前旅程。

目前核對嘅 App 版本不設帳戶、發布者伺服器或雲端同步；本機旅程不會自動傳送至發布者。App 亦不內置廣告、使用分析或跨 App 追蹤 SDK。若往後版本改變資料處理方式，我哋會更新 App 行為及本政策。

你選擇分享、匯出或開地圖時

  • 行程分享：你可以預覽再使用 iOS 分享功能。內容會包括旅程名稱、日期、活動時間、標題及地點等。私人備註只會喺你明確選擇包含時加進分享內容。分享出去係當時嘅副本，之後修改不會同步；接收者及你選用嘅分享服務按其政策處理副本。
  • 備份檔案：你可把單個旅程匯出成 JSON 檔案。備份包括該旅程及活動嘅所有已保存欄位，包括私人備註、識別碼及時間；匯入前會預覽，確認後會建立新旅程。檔案不由旅序設定獨立密碼或加密；你選擇嘅 Files 位置及其雲端服務，按各自嘅資料政策保存檔案。
  • 地圖：按下地圖連結時，App 會把你所選活動嘅地址或地點搜尋文字交給 Apple Maps 或系統開啟嘅地圖服務。該服務可能按其私隱政策處理搜尋及連線資料。
  • 複製常用語：使用複製功能時，所選韓語句子會交給 iOS 系統剪貼簿；如你啟用了 Apple Universal Clipboard，系統可能同步剪貼簿內容至其他 Apple 裝置。

保存、刪除及裝置備份

App 內旅程會留喺裝置，直至你在 App 使用「清除所有本機資料」、個別刪除，或移除 App。清除功能只清除 App 內旅程資料；不會撤回他人已收到嘅分享內容、不會刪除你另外儲存嘅備份、不會清空剪貼簿，亦不代表安全抹除裝置上所有暫存。iOS 裝置備份可能另有副本，視乎你嘅裝置設定及所用服務。

本 App 不會由發布者帳戶收取旅程資料，所以目前沒有旅程資料可由發布者代為查閱或刪除。請直接管理你分享出去嘅副本、備份檔及裝置備份。

Information the app handles

TripOrder lets you build trips on your device. Trip names, destinations, dates, time zones, activity times and titles, places, manually entered flight or accommodation details, and notes are stored in the app’s local data. The app also stores trip and activity identifiers, creation or modification times, and the trip you selected as current.

The audited app version has no account, publisher-operated server, or cloud sync. Local trip data is not automatically sent to the Publisher. The app contains no advertising, analytics, or cross-app tracking SDK. If a later version changes these practices, we will update the app and this policy.

When you choose to share, export, or open a map

  • Sharing a trip: You can preview the text and then use the iOS sharing feature. It may include the trip name, dates, activity times, titles, and places. Private notes are included only when you explicitly choose to include them. A shared trip is a snapshot and does not sync later changes. Recipients and the service you choose handle their copy under their own policies.
  • Backup files: You can export one trip as a JSON file. It contains the trip and its saved activity fields, including private notes, identifiers, and times. You can preview an import; confirming creates a new trip. TripOrder does not add a separate password or encryption to the file. The Files location and any cloud provider you select handle it under their own policies.
  • Maps: When you tap a map link, the app sends the selected activity’s address or place search text to Apple Maps or the map service opened by your device. That service may handle search and connection data under its own privacy policy.
  • Copying a phrase: Copying a Korean phrase places that phrase on the iOS system clipboard. If Apple Universal Clipboard is enabled, the system may sync clipboard content to your other Apple devices.

Storage, deletion, and device backups

Trips stay in app data on your device until you use “Clear all local data,” delete individual trips, or remove the app. The clear-data action removes trip data in the app. It does not retract shared copies, delete backup files you saved elsewhere, clear the system clipboard, or promise secure erasure of every temporary copy. iOS device backups may contain separate copies depending on your settings and services.

The app does not send trip data to a Publisher account, so the Publisher currently has no trip data to access or delete for you. Manage shared copies, backup files, and device backups where you stored them.

Contacting support by email

If you email support, the Publisher receives the sender address and message to respond and resolve the issue. Do not include private trip, identity, or payment information unless needed. We keep support emails only as long as needed to handle the request and delete them when the request is resolved, unless follow-up is still needed or the law requires longer retention. Email providers, devices, or backups may retain copies under their own rules.
